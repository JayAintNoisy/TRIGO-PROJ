Features

Everything a municipal tricycle service needs

Passenger booking, driver dispatch, LGU fare control and safety monitoring — built as one platform instead of four disconnected tools.

Passenger booking

Set a drop-off, choose Standard Trike or Exclusive Ride, pick how many passengers are splitting the fare, and send the request.

One account, two roles

Switch between Passenger and Driver from the same profile — no second app, no second login, no second device.

Municipality-bounded dispatch

Rides stay inside the municipality that issued the franchise: jurisdiction radius and boundaries are checked on every request.

Transparent fare matrix

Base fare, per-kilometre rate, minimum fare, night surcharge and the exclusive-ride multiplier are configured per municipality by the LGU admin.

Live GPS tracking

Drivers stream their position while online; passengers follow the trike, each route leg and the ETA in real time.

Verified drivers only

Licence number, plate number, tricycle model and uploaded documents are reviewed before a driver can be approved and go online.

Cash and e-wallet payment

Settle in cash or through an e-wallet. Drivers publish their own payment QR codes, so funds arrive directly.

Chat and notifications

In-app messaging scoped to a single ride, plus notifications for every status change from request to completion.

Safety, ratings and receipts

SOS-oriented monitoring in the admin portal, two-way ratings after each trip, and a full fare breakdown on every receipt.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which areas does TriGo cover?

TriGo is deployed per municipality. The seeded coverage is Lapu-Lapu City and Cordova, Cebu. An LGU can onboard another municipality by adding its centre, jurisdiction radius and fare matrix in the admin portal.

How is the fare computed?

Each municipality stores its own base fare, per-kilometre rate, minimum fare, night surcharge, exclusive-ride multiplier and maximum passenger count. The estimate is shown before you confirm the booking, and the same breakdown appears on the receipt.

What payment methods are supported?

Cash and e-wallet. Drivers enable the methods they accept and upload a payment QR code, so passengers can scan and transfer without leaving the app.

How does a driver get approved?

Register in the app, submit your licence number, tricycle plate number, model and supporting documents, then wait for the LGU admin to approve the application. Only approved drivers can go online.

What if something goes wrong during a trip?

Trips can be raised to the safety desk through the in-app support channel, and the admin portal surfaces SOS alerts, complaints and feedback for follow-up.
